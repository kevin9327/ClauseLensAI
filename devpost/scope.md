# ClauseLens Studio

One line: An interactive everyday contract x-ray and stance-calibrated counter-offer studio that spotlights hidden traps in leases, freelance agreements, and SaaS renewals and drafts ready-to-send redlines in one click.

## The Unique Kernel
Unlike generic document summarizers that return a wall of chat text, ClauseLens Studio pairs **verbatim character-span trap highlighting** with a live **3-tier Negotiation Stance Slider (`Warm & Collaborative` ↔ `Balanced Market Standard` ↔ `Firm Legal Boundary`)** that dynamically rewrites both the redlined replacement clause and the counter-offer email while calculating the exact dollar Exposure Saved.

## Who It's For
Independent freelance designers, first-time apartment renters, and solo founders who receive 6–15 page standard-form contracts ("sign by Friday") and cannot afford a $450/hr contract attorney for a $3,000 freelance gig or $2,200/month lease, yet regularly lose thousands of dollars to silent auto-renewal windows, unlimited IP assignment traps, and non-refundable deposit clauses.

## The Core Loop
1. **Load or Paste Agreement**: Select one of 3 real-world contract presets (Residential Apartment Lease, Creator/Freelance Master Services Agreement, or SaaS Annual Enterprise Renewal) or paste a custom agreement.
2. **Inspect Verbatim Trap Spans**: Click any flagged clause card (`Auto-Renewal Trap`, `Unbounded IP Grab`, `Uncapped Late Fee / Liability`, `Unilateral Termination`) to jump directly to the highlighted character span in the contract viewer and inspect its plain-English risk score and dollar exposure.
3. **Calibrate Negotiation Stance**: Toggle the Stance Slider between `Warm & Collaborative`, `Balanced Market Standard`, and `Firm Legal Boundary` to watch the redline diff (`- original trap` / `+ protective replacement`) and tone adapt in real time.
4. **Accept Redlines & Export Counter-Proposal**: Toggle which clauses to negotiate, watch the live **Dollar Exposure Saved ($)** and **Contract Fairness Score (0–100)** update, and copy the ready-to-send counter-offer email + redline rider.

## Inspiration & Identity
Inspired by Stripe Radar's precision telemetry and Linear's crisp dark-mode craft (`#090b10` obsidian slate, `#10b981` protective emerald, `#f59e0b` caution amber, `#f43f5e` trap rose, paired with `Space Grotesk` + `JetBrains Mono`). The tone is calm, empowering, and jargon-free—like having a sharp, practical senior friend review your contract before you sign.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Every builder and freelancer signs leases, client agreements, and software terms without reading the dangerous boilerplates buried in Section 14. Building ClauseLens Studio teaches how to structure deterministic text-span indexing, state-driven UI diffing, and AI-assisted clause rewriting into a cohesive product using the official `learn-ai-basics` workflow.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a 60-second walkthrough: the user opens ClauseLens Studio, clicks the **Freelance Creator Agreement** preset, sees 4 red-flag traps immediately highlighted in the contract pane (`$14,800` total financial exposure, Fairness Score `34/100`), clicks the **Unlimited Moral & Derivative IP Grab** clause, slides the Negotiation Stance from `Warm` to `Firm Legal Boundary`, toggles all 4 protective redlines on (boosting Fairness Score to `94/100`), and copies the complete counter-offer email with inline redline diffs.

## The POC Boundary
Included in this PoC:
- 3 rich, realistic multi-clause contract presets (Apartment Lease, Freelance Creator MSA, SaaS Auto-Renewal Order Form) plus custom contract paste & live trap scanner.
- Interactive split-pane Contract X-Ray Viewer with exact character-span highlighting and risk breakdown cards.
- 3-tier Negotiation Stance Slider (`Warm`, `Balanced`, `Firm`) that rewrites both clause redlines and the counter-offer email draft.
- Live Dollar Exposure Saved & Contract Fairness Score calculator (`0–100`) plus Light/Dark theme persistence.

## Later
- Multi-party PDF DocuSign envelope ingestion and browser extension overlay.
- Jurisdiction-specific statutory citation lookup (e.g., California Civil Code § 1950.5 security deposit caps).

## Explicitly Cut
- **User accounts and cloud database storage**: Cut so anyone can test sensitive contract text immediately with zero sign-up friction and complete local privacy.
- **Open-ended legal chat bot**: Cut because open-ended chat encourages vague summaries instead of actionable, copy-pasteable clause redlines.
